## 1. Clarify your idea

**What specific problem does it solve?**
_Not: "I want a social network." Yes: "I want my 5-person team to see who's working on what task without having to ask."_



**For whom, exactly?**
_The more specific, the better — specificity is an advantage, not a limitation._



**What's the minimum moment of value?**
_The first instant the user experiences the benefit. Everything else can wait._
